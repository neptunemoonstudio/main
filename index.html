About the Artist

Welcome to Neptune Moon Studio—my living art archive. Working from my home studio, I create intuitive, maximalist paintings, miniature ACEO art cards, and hand-poured scented candles.

Alongside my current canvases and candles, this site preserves my visual journey across decades, including early studio works, floral and location photography, and experimental photo collages.
